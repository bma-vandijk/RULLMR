  <h1>Responsible Use of Large Language Models in Research</h1>
  <p class="lede" markdown="1">
    Large language models (LLMs) can support scientific research in many ways. This course helps researchers to use LLMs critically, effectively, and responsibly throughout the research cycle, by providing both more background on LLMs, and practical sessions on using LLMs to find literature, analyse data, review papers, and so on. Participants are challenged to think about the ethics in using AI in a health context and staying responsible for their work.
  </p>
