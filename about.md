## What RULLMR is

Large language models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT and similar tools, are increasingly used in research. **Responsible Use of Large Language Models in Research** (RULLMR) is an eight-session course that helps researchers decide when these tools add value, when they do not, and how to remain accountable for quality, integrity, and traceability.

The course is not a tutorial for a single product. It offers a practical framework you can take back to your own projects, whether you are new to LLMs or already using them.

<div class="callout">
  <strong>No AI background required</strong>
  Sessions start from research practice, not from computer science. Technical ideas are explained in plain language: what a model is doing when it “answers”, why the same question can yield different text, and where that matters for science.
</div>

## Who it is for

All research staff: PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, research assistants, data analysts, and senior researchers. The course is relevant across disciplines.

## How sessions work

Each session combines individual preparation, discussion with a fellow participant, group discussion, practical exercises, and reflection. You will work with realistic research scenarios and consider how the methods and safeguards apply in your own context.

The emphasis throughout is on:

- safe and purposeful use of LLMs
- careful checking of outputs
- transparent documentation
- meaningful human oversight

LLMs are treated as supportive tools, not as substitutes for scientific judgement, methodological expertise, or researcher responsibility.

## Duration

The course runs over **three months**.
