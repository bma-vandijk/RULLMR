  <h1 class="post-title">About</h1>
</header>

<div class="post-content">

Large language models such as ChatGPT are increasingly used in research. RULLMR helps researchers use them critically, effectively, and responsibly across the research cycle.

The course is practice-oriented: eight sessions covering literature, study design, data, analysis, writing, peer review, and grant writing. It is not a tutorial for one product.

It is a framework for when LLMs add value, when they do not, and how you stay accountable. No computer-science background is required.

Participants remain responsible for the quality, integrity, and traceability of their work. LLMs are treated as supportive tools, not as substitutes for scientific judgement.

The course is intended for research staff at any career stage and from any discipline.
